7. Privacy & POPIA

We take the privacy of your information seriously and aim to handle personal information in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

What we collect.

  • Account information you give us: your name, email address and password (stored only in encrypted/hashed form), and your business branding details.
  • Job information you enter: client details, site addresses, photos, findings, line items and totals.
  • Basic technical information needed to run and secure the service.

Why we process it. We use this information only to provide the service to you: to sign you in, to store and display your job cards, and to generate your reports and quotes. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

Your role under POPIA. When you capture your clients' personal information in SiteWise, you are the party responsible for that information ("responsible party") towards your clients. You should make sure you have a lawful reason to collect it and, where needed, tell your clients how you use it. SiteWise acts as an operator that stores and processes that information on your behalf and under your instruction.

How we protect it. Passwords are stored using one-way hashing. Access to your data requires signing in to your account, and each business can only see its own information. We use reasonable technical measures to protect information, though no online service can promise perfect security.

How long we keep it. We keep your information for as long as your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we will remove your personal information, except anything we're required to keep by law.

Your rights. Under POPIA you have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected, and to have it deleted, subject to any legal obligations we have to retain it. To make a request, contact us using the details below.
